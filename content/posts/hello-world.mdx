Welcome to my new portfolio and blog! I've been meaning to set up a personal site for a while now, and I'm excited to finally have a place to share my thoughts and work.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Development**: Tips, tricks, and lessons learned from building web applications

I wanted a simple, fast, and minimalist place to share my work and ideas. This site is built with Next.js and focuses on typography and readability.
