Minimalism in design isn't about removing everything—it's about removing everything that doesn't serve a purpose.

## The Power of Constraints

When you limit your design palette, you're forced to focus on what really matters:

- **Typography**: Good typefaces and hierarchy can carry an entire design
- **Whitespace**: Breathing room makes content more digestible
- **Color**: A limited palette creates visual cohesion

## Content First

A minimal design puts the focus where it belongs: on your content. Without flashy animations or complex layouts, readers can focus on what you're actually saying.

## Performance Benefits

Minimal designs are also typically faster. Less CSS, fewer images, and simpler layouts mean faster load times and better user experience.

## Finding Balance

The challenge is finding the right balance. You want enough visual interest to be engaging, but not so much that it distracts from your message.

For me, that balance comes through typography, thoughtful spacing, and letting the content shine.
